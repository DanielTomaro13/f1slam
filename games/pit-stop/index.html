The clock is the tyre change. Name as many real F1 drivers as you can in 60 seconds — every correct name is one more on the board.

How to play

  1. Hit start and the 60-second clock begins.
  2. Type a driver's surname (or full name) and submit — correct names light up green.
  3. Each driver only counts once; spelling is forgiving on accents.
  4. When the clock hits zero, post your total to the Hall of Fame.
